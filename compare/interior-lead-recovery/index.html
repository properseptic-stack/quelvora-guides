How to read the difference

  • If a basic auto-reply is all you need and you prefer to optimize for the lowest public SaaS price, the first two options are the relevant starting points.
  • Interior Lead Recovery is intentionally positioned higher: you are buying a local, done-for-you install and walkthrough for an Interior BC shop, not just access to a generic software dashboard.
  • Ask every provider what number, forwarding, consent language, booking flow, CRM handoff, support, message limits, and cancellation terms are included for your specific business.
