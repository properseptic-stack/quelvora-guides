When a third-party GaN brick wins

  • You charge phone + laptop + earbuds from one outlet (hotel / desk).
  • You need 45W–100W+ USB-C PD for a MacBook.
  • You’re willing to stick to reputable brands (Anker, Belkin, Ugreen, etc.) with clear PD specs and Canadian returns.

Avoid the cheapest no-name “20W” clones with no safety certifications. A bad brick can damage cables or, rarely, create heat hazards — not worth saving a few dollars.
