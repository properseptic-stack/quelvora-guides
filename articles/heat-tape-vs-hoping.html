Quick take (pin): BC Interior freeze tip: self-regulating heat cable + foam on the vulnerable run beats another cracked fitting. Measure first. GFCI. CO alarm if you run a generator.

I’m in the BC Interior (trades + septic). When an exposed supply line freezes, you’re not just out of water — well pumps, pressure tanks, and (on many rural places) anything downstream that expects flow all take a hit. What actually helps before the deep cold:

  1. Find the vulnerable run — outdoor hose bibs, crawlspace lines, the last stretch into a pump house or crawl.
  2. Self-regulating heat cable on the pipe (not random “heat tape” with no listing marks) + foam insulation over it. Measure length and pipe OD first.
  3. GFCI-protected outlet for the cable. If you’re unsure about the circuit, hire a licensed electrician.
  4. If you run a generator in winter, put a CO alarm where people sleep — generators kill quietly.
