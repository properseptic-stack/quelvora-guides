What it is

PanOxyl Acne Wash with 4% benzoyl peroxide is a face-and-body wash format. The label positions it to help clear and prevent acne and help clear pores. Always read your bottle for the current directions and warnings.

Simple routine people follow

  1. Patch test on a small area first if you are new to benzoyl peroxide — it can dry or irritate.
  2. Wet skin, work a small amount into a lather on face and/or body areas you actually want to wash, then rinse.
  3. Start slow (every other day) if your skin runs sensitive; increase only if it stays comfortable.
  4. Moisturize after — BP washes can strip.
  5. Daytime sunscreen matters if you are using BP; many routines pair wash + moisturizer + SPF.
  6. Keep it away from fabrics you care about — benzoyl peroxide can bleach towels and pillowcases.

When to stop and get advice

If you get severe irritation, swelling, or breathing issues, stop and seek medical care. Persistent or cystic acne needs a clinician — a wash is not a prescription plan.
