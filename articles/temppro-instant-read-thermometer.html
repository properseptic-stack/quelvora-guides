A fast digital thermometer (example: TempPro TempSwift instant-read) is a common Amazon.ca kitchen gadget.

Why people buy one

Guessing doneness wastes food. Instant-read probes help check burgers, chicken, candy stages, and more in a few seconds.

Simple routine

  1. Insert into the thickest part (avoid bone).
  2. Wait for the reading to settle.
  3. Clean the probe after each use.
  4. Calibrate if your model supports it / as the manual says.
