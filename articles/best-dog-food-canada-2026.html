What “best” should mean in Canada

  • Complete & balanced for the life stage on the bag (AAFCO or equivalent statement).
  • Company feeding trials / board-certified nutritionists when possible (Purina, Hills, Royal Canin are the usual names).
  • Availability at PetSmart, Pet Valu, Amazon.ca, or your local shop so you can reorder without panic.
  • Price per day, not just price per bag — a 15.9 kg bag often wins on cost-per-meal.

How to switch without GI drama

  1. Day 1–2: 25% new / 75% old
  2. Day 3–4: 50 / 50
  3. Day 5–6: 75 / 25
  4. Day 7+: full new diet

Slow down if stool softens. Keep fresh water available. Measure with a cup — “free feed” hides overfeeding.

Quick FAQ

Is grain-free automatically better? No. Many healthy dogs do well on grain-inclusive diets; choose based on your dog and vet guidance, not marketing.

Is Costco / Kirkland OK? Some owners love it; others prefer brands with more published feeding-trial data. If budget is primary, compare labels and talk to your vet.

Where should Canadians buy? Local specialty shops for advice; Amazon.ca / big-box for convenience and Subscribe & Save style reorders when the price is right.
