Quick take: BC septic myth — additives ≠ maintenance. Risers for winter access + a clean effluent filter beat monthly chemicals. Measure your lid before you buy.

I’m a ROWP in the BC Interior. Short version: monthly additives aren’t a substitute for pumping and the maintenance plan that goes with your system, and some products can work against a healthy tank. What usually helps homeowners more:

  1. Know your lids in winter — if you’re digging every service call, a riser to grade saves money and keeps snow/compact from sitting on the cover.
  2. Keep the effluent filter clean (if you have one) on the schedule your plan calls for.
  3. Pump when the plan says — not when a bottle says you’ll never need to.
