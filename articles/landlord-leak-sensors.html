Quick take (pin): Landlord tip: put leak sensors under sinks and by the water heater before the next soaked floor. Cheap alert > deductible.

Small-landlord note from BC: the expensive calls aren’t always furnaces — they’re slow leaks under sinks, behind toilets, and beside water heaters that soak flooring for weeks. Cheap sensors beat another insurance deductible:

  1. Put a leak sensor under kitchen/bath sinks, beside the water heater, and at the washing machine shutoff.
  2. Watch basement humidity in suites — mould disputes start with “we didn’t know it was wet.”
  3. Test after install and note the date in your property file.
  4. Sensors alert you; they don’t fix plumbing — still call a plumber when they scream.
