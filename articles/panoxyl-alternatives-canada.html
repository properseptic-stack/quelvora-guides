Alternative paths (same job, different product)

1. Other benzoyl peroxide washes / gels

Same active, different brand or strength (2.5%–10%). Lower % can be gentler; gels leave residue that some prefer for spot use. Check the Drug Facts / medicinal ingredients panel on Canadian packaging.

2. Salicylic acid (BHA) cleansers

Better for blackheads / oily texture for some people; different mechanism than BP. Examples people compare in Canada include CeraVe SA cleansers and The Ordinary salicylic products — buy what matches your skin and label instructions, not TikTok hype alone.

3. Gentle cleanser + leave-on actives

If washes strip you, a bland cleanser plus a leave-on BP gel or adapalene (where appropriate / clinician-guided) can be easier to control than a strong wash.

4. Hydrocolloid patches for individual spots

Not a PanOxyl substitute for antibacterial wash jobs — complementary for picking / overnight spots. See hydrocolloid spot covers notes.

Bleach warning (all BP products)

Benzoyl peroxide can bleach towels, pillowcases, and coloured fabrics. Use white towels for wash nights or rinse thoroughly. This applies to PanOxyl and generic BP.

Canada shopping tip

Shoppers’ Drug Mart, Rexall, Walmart, and Amazon.ca usually stock PanOxyl or near-equivalents. Compare unit price (¢/g) and % BP — the “alternative” is often just another 4% bottle.
