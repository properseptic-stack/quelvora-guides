Quick take (pin): Landlord winter checklist: test smoke + CO before the furnace season. Replace end-of-life units; document the date.

Small-landlord note from BC: smoke and CO duties aren’t optional paperwork — they’re the cheap insurance that keeps a cold-season outage or a furnace issue from becoming a tribunal nightmare. Before the heat kicks hard (or at tenancy change):

  1. Test every unit — press the button; replace anything that fails or is past end-of-life.
  2. Match the install type — hardwired interconnect vs battery. Don’t guess; look at what’s already there.
  3. CO where fuel-burning appliances or attached garages exist — and anywhere a portable heater or generator might get used.
  4. Document the test date in your file / condition report.
