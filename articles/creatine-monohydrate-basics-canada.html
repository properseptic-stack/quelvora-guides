Creatine monohydrate basics for gym beginners (Canada)

A lot of Amazon.ca shoppers grab micronized creatine because it is a simple gym staple — not a miracle powder. Here is a practical, label-faithful overview.

What it usually is

Creatine monohydrate powder (example: Jacked Factory 425g micronized, unflavoured, about 85 servings) mixes with water or a shake. Always read your label for serving size and certifications.

Simple routine people follow

  1. Use the serving size on the label (often about 5g).
  2. Mix in water or a post-workout drink; micronized mixes easier.
  3. Take it consistently on training and rest days if that matches how you prefer to use it.
  4. Drink normal fluids through the day.
  5. Skip aggressive loading schemes unless a coach you trust prescribed them.

What this is not

Not a steroid. Not a fat burner. Not a substitute for training, protein, or sleep.
