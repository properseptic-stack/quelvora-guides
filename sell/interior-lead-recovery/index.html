Missed calls shouldn’t become someone else’s jobs

Interior Lead Recovery installs a simple missed-call text-back for plumbers, HVAC, electrical, and similar shops. When a call goes unanswered, the caller gets a friendly text with a booking link — usually in under 30 seconds — and you get a note so the lead isn’t lost.

The problem (plain English)

You’re on a job. The phone rings. You can’t answer. That person often dials the next shop on Google. By the time you listen to voicemail, the work is gone.

This is for your business phone — people who already called you. Not cold spam. Not septic ads. Not medical claims.

What we set up

  1. Missed / unanswered call is detected
  2. Caller gets a short text with who you are + a book-a-time link + STOP to opt out
  3. Contact + note lands in a simple CRM list so you can follow up
  4. You get a short walkthrough so you know how to reply
